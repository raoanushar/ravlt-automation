Executive - Sentence Completion

Section 12: Sentence Completion

Complete the sentences with words that do not make sense in context. Take the first answer; do not allow self-correction. If a contextual answer is given, remind once that the response must be unrelated.

Say

“Listen carefully to these sentences and as soon as I have finished reading them, please tell me a word that finishes the sentence as quickly as possible but does not make sense. For example, ‘John cut his hand with the sharp…orange’.”

If no response within 20 seconds, move to the next question. Use the first answer only.
